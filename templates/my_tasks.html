How these numbers are calculated
Total Tasks — all active tasks assigned to you (soft-deleted tasks are excluded).
Completed — tasks with status Done.
Completion Rate — Completed ÷ Total × 100.
On Time — Done tasks that have a due date, where the completion timestamp's date ≤ due date.
Late — Done tasks that have a due date, where the completion timestamp's date > due date.
Done tasks without a due date are counted in Completed but not in On Time or Late.
